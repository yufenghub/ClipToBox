隐私政策

ClipToBox Privacy Policy · 生效日期：2026-08-10

ClipToBox 的核心用途是检测剪贴板中新出现的图片，并将其粘贴到用户允许的 AI 对话网页。

1. 我们处理什么数据

ClipToBox 需要访问剪贴板中的图片，以识别新截图或新复制的图片并执行粘贴功能。扩展还会在浏览器本地保存必要的设置，例如总开关、自动粘贴设置、当前标签页/多标签页设置、启用的网站以及用户添加的网站配置。

2. 图片如何被使用

剪贴板图片仅用于实现 ClipToBox 的图片粘贴功能。当功能触发时，图片会被提供给用户当前选择或允许的 AI 对话网页，例如 ChatGPT、Gemini、Claude、DeepSeek、Qwen 或豆包。第三方 AI 网站对收到的数据如何处理，由各网站自己的隐私政策和服务条款决定。

3. 我们不做什么

  • ClipToBox 不运营用于收集剪贴板图片或聊天内容的开发者数据服务器。
  • 我们不会将剪贴板图片保存到开发者控制的服务器。
  • 我们不会出售用户数据。
  • 我们不会将用户数据用于广告、跨站跟踪、用户画像、信用评估或与扩展核心用途无关的目的。
  • ClipToBox 不会为了其核心功能而读取用户的 AI 聊天历史。

4. 网站访问权限

ClipToBox 默认支持若干 AI 对话网站，并允许用户自行添加其他 HTTPS 网站。扩展只在内置支持或由用户明确授权的网站中执行相关脚本。用户可以在扩展设置中关闭单个网站或关闭整个扩展。

5. 本地设置

扩展设置通过浏览器扩展存储保存在本地浏览器环境中，用于记住用户的功能偏好和网站配置。这些设置不会由 ClipToBox 开发者主动上传到自有服务器。

6. 第三方服务

ClipToBox 本身不提供 AI 对话服务。用户将图片粘贴到第三方 AI 网站后，该网站可能根据其自身政策处理相关内容。建议用户查阅相应第三方服务的隐私政策。

7. 政策更新

如果 ClipToBox 的数据处理方式发生实质变化，本页面会相应更新，并修改生效日期。

8. 联系方式

如对本隐私政策或 ClipToBox 有疑问，可以通过 GitHub Issues 联系项目维护者。

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 10, 2026

ClipToBox is designed to detect newly captured or copied images in the clipboard and paste them into AI chat webpages explicitly allowed by the user.

1. Data processed by ClipToBox

ClipToBox accesses clipboard images in order to detect a newly captured or copied image and perform the requested paste function. The extension also stores necessary preferences locally in the browser, such as the master switch, automatic paste preference, current-tab or multi-tab preference, enabled websites, and custom website configuration.

2. How clipboard images are used

Clipboard images are used only to provide ClipToBox's image-paste functionality. When the feature is triggered, the image is provided to an AI chat webpage selected or allowed by the user, such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, DeepSeek, Qwen, or Doubao. Data subsequently handled by those third-party services is subject to their own privacy policies and terms.

3. What ClipToBox does not do

  • ClipToBox does not operate a developer data server for collecting clipboard images or AI chat content.
  • Clipboard images are not stored on servers controlled by the ClipToBox developer.
  • User data is not sold.
  • User data is not used for advertising, cross-site tracking, profiling, creditworthiness, lending, or purposes unrelated to the extension's single purpose.
  • ClipToBox does not read AI chat history for its core functionality.

4. Website access

ClipToBox includes a set of supported AI chat websites and allows users to add additional HTTPS websites. The extension runs its relevant scripts only on built-in supported websites or websites explicitly authorized by the user. Users can disable individual websites or disable the entire extension at any time.

5. Local settings

Extension preferences are stored in the browser's extension storage so that ClipToBox can remember feature and website settings. ClipToBox does not intentionally upload these settings to a developer-operated server.

6. Third-party services

ClipToBox does not provide the AI chat services themselves. Once a user pastes an image into a third-party AI website, that service may process the image according to its own privacy policy. Users should review the privacy practices of the relevant third-party service.

7. Changes to this policy

If ClipToBox materially changes how it handles data, this page will be updated and the effective date will be revised.

8. Contact

For privacy or support questions, please contact the project maintainer through GitHub Issues.
